High school senior in Colombia 🇨🇴. I build things where **math, finance and AI** meet, and I like taking a project all the way from the question to working code.

### 🔭 What I'm working on
- **[Less Diversified, Riskier, or Both?](https://github.com/ianjacobo2009-hub/sp500-paper)**: a research paper on how the AI giants changed the S&P 500. I froze the index at ChatGPT's launch to measure what their growth did to diversification and risk, using 28 SEC filings and 9 years of daily prices.
- **Arthur**: my personal AI assistant, with voice chat, a morning briefing, calendar and email triage, and control of my TV. Built with Python, shell and Claude.
- **Rescue Map**: an app that maps emergency help routes in Cali.

### 🏔️ Blue Peak AI & Finance Solutions
I founded Blue Peak to help small businesses use AI automation and better financial tools.

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🛡️ **[COPGuard](https://github.com/ianjacobo2009-hub/copguard)** | COP/USD exchange-rate risk: GARCH volatility, 10,000-path Monte Carlo VaR, hedging and stress tests on 20 years of official data |
| 📈 **[Market Pulse](https://github.com/ianjacobo2009-hub/marketpulse)** | Daily market dashboard: 20 live markets, a fear–greed score, 7 quantitative signals and an AI-written summary |
| 💳 **[BlueCredit](https://github.com/ianjacobo2009-hub/bluecredit)** | Spanish loan simulator: the real effective annual rate, a debt-to-income check with all your debts, a usury warning and a PDF summary |
| 🏠 **[MortgageIQ](https://github.com/ianjacobo2009-hub/mortgageiq)** | Mortgage calculator for Colombia and the USA in Spanish and English: correct E.A. and UVR math, PMI/FHA/ARM, affordability checks, rent vs buy and a PDF summary |
